# Portfolio image sources

The DineSense images are existing public app previews from Zander Erwin's DineSense website, downloaded October 16, 2026. They have not been generated or presented as live production sessions.

- `dinesense-order.webp`: https://dinesense.app/assets/previews/edited-order-880.webp
- `dinesense-results.webp`: https://dinesense.app/assets/previews/results-440.webp
- `dinesense-target.webp`: https://dinesense.app/assets/previews/target-440.webp

The portrait in `src/images/portrait-sm.jpg` and the existing application icons are supplied assets retained from deployed source commit `0e5871b`.

The architectural illustrations are original SVG/CSS in `src/components/SystemSketch.jsx` and `src/App.js`. The Programmed widget is a browser recreation with demonstration workout data, labeled in the interface.

## Programmed

`programmed-workout.png` is the actual dark workout screenshot from the owner's local app repository, `The-Locker/docs/appstore/02-workout-dark.png`, copied without modification on 2026-10-16. The app is in development.

The interactive Live Activity is a browser adaptation based on `LockerUI/Components/SystemSurfaces.swift` (WorkoutActivityCard, ActivityPill), `Theme/LockerColor.swift`, `Theme/LockerType.swift`, and the captured `docs/redesign/reports/phase5-live-activity-rest.png`. The demo's sample sets and pause control are local portfolio behavior. Its Barlow Condensed and Archivo fonts come from the app's bundled, OFL-licensed assets; licenses are in `public/fonts/`.
