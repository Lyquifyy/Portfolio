# Design System: Zander Erwin Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Engineering Exhibition"**

The portfolio makes software construction visible. Saturated cobalt frames pale, spacious work presentations; oversized, tightly set type supplies the confidence. An exploded software stack, real product screens, and a labeled interactive Live Activity adaptation provide the visual substance. Fine rules and straight-edged controls keep the surrounding interface precise.

This is the current code-led system for software engineering hiring teams. Authority is `public/index.html`, `src/index.css`, `src/App.css`, `src/App.js`, the three artifact components, and `src/hooks/useRequestTrace.js`. Product truth belongs in `PRODUCT.md`; page strategy belongs in `.impeccable/surfaces/src-app-js.md`.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cobalt framing with restrained lime emphasis, selection, and focus.
- Bricolage Grotesque headings and quiet Manrope body text.
- One neutral stage for every project, with product identity inside the artifact.
- Real screens and clearly labeled illustrative interactions.
- Finite motion that explains a request or confirms an action.
- Native scrolling and disclosures, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.

## Colors

Cobalt, cool paper, ink, and lime establish the site identity. The frontmatter records actual source values, including named component treatments; those treatments are not a general-purpose tonal scale.

### Primary

Cobalt frames navigation, hero, and contact, and marks selected preview controls and active architecture nodes. Deep cobalt supports the mobile navigation and light-button text. Lime emphasizes the final hero line, selected stack layers, focus on cobalt, and action feedback.

### Neutral

Paper is the page background. The shared stage is the same cool neutral for DineSense, Programmed, and ExpenseTracker. Ink, muted text, and fine line colors establish hierarchy. Experience gray distinguishes the career section. Frame alpha colors, sketch geometry colors, career status colors, and device-border colors are scoped to their named components.

**The Artifact Palette Rule.** All project outer fields use the shared stage. Product-specific colors belong inside the artifact; Programmed’s source-derived bone, charcoal, red, and lit-indicator colors do not become site palette choices.

Programmed’s literal colors remain in its component tokens and specimen CSS. Its actual screenshot is copied unchanged from the local `The-Locker/docs/appstore/02-workout-dark.png`; the adaptation uses the app’s LockerUI v4 + Lit treatment. There are no separate sage, slate, or mist project fields.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Bricolage Grotesque, sans-serif. **Body Font:** Manrope, sans-serif. Both are self-hosted WOFF2, preloaded in `public/index.html`, with variable ranges 400–800 and 400–700 and `font-display: swap`.

Programmed alone uses self-hosted Barlow Condensed 700/800 (CSS alias `Programmed Barlow`) and Archivo 400 (alias `Programmed Archivo`), copied from its actual local app source. The condensed face carries the wordmark, exercise, prescription, timer, and buttons; Archivo carries supporting metadata. Preserve that distinction inside the artifact.

The frontmatter enumerates observed roles and responsive sizes, not a fabricated modular scale. Desktop, compact, stacked, and mobile display values follow the four authored layouts. Section, project, career, biography, contact, and artifact headings retain their individual roles. Repeated small sizes cover metadata, native disclosures, and diagram annotations; do not promote them to primary prose.

Project descriptions stop at 52ch on desktop, hero copy at 42ch, and biography copy at 51ch. Responsive descriptions extend to 65ch when projects stack. Weight and line-height still follow the component source where a role records only its size.

**The Title First Rule.** Project and employer names lead their information block. Category, year, and employment dates sit beneath the corresponding heading.

## Layout

The shared content width is `min(100% - var(--gutter) * 2, 1320px)`. Work uses 110px vertical padding and 88px between featured projects on desktop; spacing is authored by context, with no invented spacing scale.

The hero pairs equal copy and drawing columns. Its redundant status/location/scroll footer strip has been removed. Featured projects pair a 1.14fr artifact with 1fr copy, reversing Programmed on desktop. Every artifact uses the same bordered outer stage. Career uses a 1fr / 1.2fr grid and a sticky introduction at 50px; additional work uses ruled native disclosure rows.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat except for product artifacts. Phone/screenshots share `0 24px 36px -16px #17202b55`; the Live Activity uses `0 20px 40px -16px #0008`. Its unlit bulbs use an inset `0 1px 2px #0008`. Keep these treatments local to the device and app material. The hero’s depth comes from SVG geometry and separated layers.

## Shapes

Site controls, tags, project stages, and architecture nodes remain straight-edged with fine 1px rules. Circular geometry marks small status/indicator dots and the contact arrow.

DineSense device corners are 30px, then 26px at compact width and 23px on mobile. Programmed’s screenshot has 32px corners, 27px on mobile; its Live Activity retains 24px corners and pill-shaped 30px controls. These describe actual devices and native app material, not a universal radius scale. The portrait remains an unframed image whose crop shifts from 4:5 to 3:4 to square.

## Components

### Actions and navigation

The white work anchor has a 50px minimum height and lime hover state. Text actions underline on hover and shift their SVG arrow slightly. Navigation underlines the visible section; its small-screen Menu/Close control supports expanded state, Escape, focus return, and closing after selection. Résumé and contact routes remain direct.

Global focus is a 3px outline with 6px offset; cobalt sections use lime. The app artifact uses its own bone focus outline with a 3px offset. Keep the keyboard-visible skip link. Technology tags are static bordered list items.

### Project stages and disclosures

All three outer stages share the same surface, border, and responsive heights. DineSense retains its real overlapping screenshots, an opaque stage-colored control strip, cobalt pressed state, and visible attribution. Screen changes use a short lateral/rotation entrance; screenshots retain descriptive alternative text.

Engineering notes, other projects, and job details use native `details` / `summary` with plus/minus markers. No custom modal is required.

### Software stack and architecture trace

The hero has three inspectable layers, initially selecting Logic. Native pressed-state buttons stop a running trace and restore manual selection. Explanation text and tools update with the selection. ExpenseTracker uses the same request grammar across client, API, and database; its nodes are paper at rest and cobalt only while active. The diagram remains labeled Architecture overview.

`useRequestTrace` runs five 680ms phases (`0 → 1 → 2 → 1 → 0`), for 3.4 seconds total. Each artifact auto-runs once on entering view, and its labeled button can replay or restart it. Leaving view, hiding the document, changing to reduced motion, or manually selecting a stack layer stops and clears the sequence. An interrupted sequence does not report completion. Completed runs show a stable summary until interrupted or restarted; manual replay under reduced motion skips movement and immediately shows the completion summary. Initial reduced-motion entry keeps the static explanatory state.

### Programmed Live Activity adaptation

The unchanged real workout screenshot sits behind a labeled interactive adaptation, with the caption “Actual app screen + interactive Live Activity adaptation.” The app’s charcoal surface, bone text, condensed type, red action, and lit completion bulbs are scoped to this artifact. Sample Back Squat data is local demonstration data.

Log four sets, run a 90-second rest countdown between sets, pause/resume or skip rest, and try again after completion. Set bulbs, count text, a progress track, and status announcements communicate state; the labeled timer does not announce every tick. This is not live activity from a user account or a claim of released availability.

### Motion and preferences

**Revision: motion runs on its own.** The page no longer waits for a click. A single scroll loop writes each block's progress to `--p`; headings rise word by word, prose reads in, stages and copy stagger, and everything scrubs in both directions. The stack's request pass, the DineSense screen cycle, the Programmed session and the ExpenseTracker trace all play while on screen and pause briefly when touched. A rail in the left gutter maps the page as a request path. Wheel input eases on fine pointers; touch, keyboard and the scrollbar stay native. The paragraphs below describe the per-piece timings that still apply.


The headline reveals once over .8s. Stack layers assemble over 1.1s with .12s/.06s delays; face/stroke feedback takes .4s. Request connectors travel for .68s per phase. Screenshot changes take .55s, completed bulbs .35s, and rest-track updates .35s linear. Link arrows and controls use short .15–.25s transitions. These are finite explanations and local feedback, with no perpetual loop or added dependency.

Reduced motion globally disables animation and transitions and preserves content, controls, and static layer positions. Forced colors maps status markers and the sketch to system colors. There is one light site theme, with the real dark product artifact inside it.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use cobalt framing, cool paper, and restrained lime selection together.
- **Do** give every project the same neutral outer stage.
- **Do** preserve Programmed’s actual typography and colors inside its artifact.
- **Do** use finite motion to explain requests, screen changes, and confirmed actions.
- **Do** preserve native disclosures, focus, labeled controls, and reduced-motion behavior.
- **Do** retain truthful project status, artifact labels, image provenance, and direct contact/source/résumé routes.

### Don't:

- **Don't** revive project-specific outer backgrounds or the removed hero footer strip.
- **Don't** spread device corners, app gradients, or artifact shadows onto the site interface.
- **Don't** hide essential content behind animation or require a pointer to explore the stack.
- **Don't** describe interrupted traces as completed or illustrative workout data as live activity.
- **Don't** invent unused token scales, tonal ramps, themes, or components.
